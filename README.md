# isomux-loop

An [isomux](https://github.com/nmamano/isomux) app (registered in the office's apps) for the continuous loop (design: isomux `internal-docs/continuous-loop-design.md`, task 178dbb5a).

- Pause stops dispatch and the tick (Isomux PM pauses around restarts and while batch work holds workers).
- Dispatch, every 2 minutes: each free Isomux Worker among Workers 1..N (N set on the status page, 1 to 6, default 2) gets the next ready board task (priority, then age), claimed with a versioned PATCH, and a `/isomux-loop-worker <task id>` message.
- Tick, hourly: idle workers that hold a task get a nudge; Isomux Main hears how many tasks wait to merge.
- A worker holds a task by agent id when the task carries one (isomux task 83f71be9), else by name: a new worker with a killed worker's name does not hold the killed one's task and counts as free. Such a task shows as "Holder killed" in the diagram, for Isomux PM to reassign or set back to ready; a claim the server refuses for a killed holder is reported apart from conflicts.
- Curator wake, every 10 minutes, also while paused: open P0-P3 tasks in the room with no tag and no card in the app go to the curator to propose, and Backlog tasks with no recommendation that Nil has not kept go to it to recommend (a curator proposal for a P4 task, any choice, is its recommendation on the Backlog tab, not a card), in one message (a task is announced again only after an hour). Every board read the app makes (status, the Triage list, packets, the Backlog, the wake; one read is shared for 20 s) withdraws the undecided cards and packets whose task is done, obsolete or gone, so a closed task leaves Triage within 20 s.
- Triage, Nil's only queue for loop work: the curator's proposals (one choice: go, design or hold ("on hold": the loop never touches it), each with a priority P0-P3 and an optional new title and scope; backlog (P4, no tag); or obsolete. Each proposal carries an effort estimate, S/M/L/XL. Nil's note can change anything) and the workers' design packets (the question as the heading, 2-4 options with their cost, one marked "recommended", the answer paragraph, the rest folded as Evidence, screenshots, branch and diff links), packets first. Nil decides a packet like a proposal; an option fills the choice and the note. Nil can also answer rewrite (with a note on what to write differently): the curator rewrites the task or packet and proposes it again, and the card comes back to Triage. Nil's decisions go to the curator in one message per burst; the curator writes them to the board and reports back.
- Each task id has a button that copies it.
- Images (sign-off screenshots and pairs, packet images) open in place in a viewer: fit to the screen, a click or tap toggles 100%, arrows or a swipe move through the item's images (a before/after pair opens as the pair, stacked on a phone), Esc, the backdrop or Close dismiss. No image opens a new tab.
- Sort and filter on Triage and Backlog: sort by proposed priority (the default), board priority, oldest or newest task, newest card, or smallest effort; filter chips for kind, proposed choice, priority, effort, change against the board priority and filed by (a packet passes every row except kind; priority matches its board priority), and a search box (/) over title, description, task id and the curator's reason. "Showing n of m" shows the filtered count; the tab and the diagram keep the full total, and the keys follow the order shown. The browser keeps the view until Clear.
- Keyboard: on Triage and Backlog one card is current (j/k or the arrows move). g d h b o w pick go/design/hold/backlog/obsolete/rewrite, 0-3 the priority, a fills in the proposal (on a packet, the recommended option; p the next option), e expands, n goes to the note (Esc leaves it), and Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac; in the note or not) confirms; Enter and Shift+Enter never confirm. ? shows the list.
- Undo: a confirmed or Later card slides out and the next one flashes; a notice offers Undo for 24 s (or Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z). Decided and Past decisions cards have Undo too. Undo puts the card back in Triage with Nil's picks and note; after the curator applied it, the card reopens and Nil's next decision overwrites the board. A Backlog decision not applied yet goes back to the Backlog. A decision the curator applied after Nil undid it still counts as applied, and the card stays open.
- Split view: on a screen at least 900 px wide, "Diagram beside" shows the task-state diagram next to Triage; it updates after each decision and every 15 s while the page shows it, and a draggable divider sets the widths. The browser keeps both choices.
- Backlog: the tasks of the Backlog state (P4, no card), oldest first. Nil decides one: go, design or hold with a priority (P0-P3), backlog to keep it, or obsolete (the note gives the reason); that is a decided card that goes to the curator like any other. The curator's recommendation (choice, priority, reason, effort; posted with `POST /api/proposals` for the P4 task) shows on the card and is preselected; it is dropped when a decision on the task is reported applied (Nil, 2026-10-23). A task Nil kept (a backlog decision reported applied) folds to the bottom as "Kept <date>", hidden behind "Show kept (N)", and the curator's recommendations for it are skipped until its title or description changes; the change drops its old recommendation, so the curator wake asks for a new one (Nil, 2026-10-23).
- On hold: each on-hold task on the Status tab (the On hold list and the diagram's On hold state) has "Back to Triage". It makes an open card (a placeholder proposal keeps the task on hold) and messages the curator to propose it; Nil decides it like any card, and the curator writes the decision over the [hold] tag. Undo, or "Put back on hold" on the card, takes the card away before he decides (Nil, 2026-10-23).
- Sign-off Scout (Nil, 2026-10-21): when a merge puts an item on the Sign-off tab, the app messages Isomux Sign-off Scout, which posts a verdict (`POST /api/signoff/:taskId/scout`): "Quick stamp" (he will likely approve as is), "Look closer" (the default) or "Push back", with a one-line reason and optional comment suggestions per entry. The card shows the verdict first and each suggestion as a "Suggested edit" block above its comment field; its Use button puts it in the field, and its text selects so Nil can copy part of it (Nil, 2026-10-23). Nothing is filled in by itself. A filter row on the Sign-off tab picks items by verdict. Nil's decisions, with the verdict each item had, stay in `GET /api/signoff/history` after the item leaves, so the Scout can compare.
- Sign-off before the push: a worker posts the copy and visible changes of a task at hand-off; Isomux Main reports every merge (and every revert) with its commit; only merged items show, so a task with no code (copy only) is reported with the current main sha when Main closes it. Each copy entry has an audience, "member" (what a person reads) or "agent" (system prompt, agent reference, skills, API errors, logs); without one the app guesses from `where` and says so. Member entries come first; agent entries are dimmer and folded to one line. Merged items show with a word diff per copy entry and their screenshots; screenshots posted as a before/after pair (`?pair=<name>&side=before|after`) show side by side like Old/New. Nil can comment on each copy entry and screenshot (a note, or the exact new text in quotes; saved as he types). One note field serves both buttons. He approves (with comments, about copy or any other change, or a note, Approve also files a P0 `[go | ready] Sign-off changes: <title>` task with them, and the item holds the push point back until that task's merge is reported or the task is closed, so a "remove this before it ships" comment never goes out with the push; the changes follow as their own commit and sign-off item), or rejects with a note that carries his comments (the app asks Main for the revert a minute later; until then the notice and the card count down, and Undo takes the rejection back, Nil, 2026-10-24). Undo on an approved item (until the push go) puts it back on Sign-off with its note; a Sign-off changes task the approval filed is closed as obsolete, with the reason in its description, if no worker has started it (open, no assignee, still `[go | ready]`), else it stays and the notice says why. An approval's note goes in that task word for word. The page keeps an unsent note and comments on the device until the decision is sent. Approved items move to the Push tab: the same tasks as the diagram's Push stage (approved items and merges with nothing to sign off). Main is linear, so a push is "everything up to sha X": the push point is the newest merge with every sign-off item at or before it approved (or nothing to sign off), and "Push up to <sha>" sends Main the push go for it, so Nil pushes along the way while later items wait. The Push tab lists what goes out and what waits, and for what. A rejected item holds the push point back until Main reports the revert, and no point between a rejected merge and its revert is used. The Sign-off tab lists items oldest merge first, so approving from the top moves the push point. Every reported merge waits in the Push state until Main reports the push of a sha that includes it (`POST /api/signoff/pushed`). The app cannot tell which member pressed a button: the office passes it no member identity.
- Status: the task states as a diagram with live counts, including Triage and Decided (exactly the open and the decided cards of the Triage and Decided tabs, whatever the board says of their tasks; the Past decisions tab is not a stage) Sign-off (merged, its sign-off item waits for Nil or for its revert) and Push (merged, not pushed yet) (tap a state to list its tasks; done and obsolete list the last 20 by close time), the sign-off count, the ready queue, holders, merging, on hold. Close times come from each task's history, cached by task version in `closed-times.json`.

The board is the loop's only state. The app keeps triage proposals, packets, sign-off items, images and the settings in `$ISOMUX_APP_DATA_DIR`. It reaches the board and other agents with the app token, which has its owner's reach since isomux task c0204eb6. Constants (room, worker names, intervals) are in `src/config.ts`.

API: `curl -s http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/api/help`. Run: `bun run start`. Tests: `bun test`.
